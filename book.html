How it works

Seats open on the first of the month.

  1. Seats open on the first

    Dates go on sale at seven in the morning Hawaii time, on the first of the month. There is no early access and nothing is held back.

  2. They go quickly

    A hundred and fifty seats, most taken the same morning. Be on the page before seven with your party size decided.

  3. Paid at the time

    Bookings are settled in full when you make them.

Themes

Every month has one.

Dressing to the theme of the month is highly encouraged — most of the room does, and the photographs are much better for it.

October
Floral. Prints, lei, a flower behind the ear. The easiest one to dress for and the one most people commit to.
November
All white. White or off-white, head to foot. It photographs extremely well against the sunset.
December
Mele Kalikimaka. Hawaiian Christmas — red, green and gold, and as much tinsel as you can justify on a beach.

Cancellations and changes.

We run in rain. A storm warning moves us to the following Monday, or you are refunded in full.
