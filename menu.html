Menu

The buffet.

The same buffet every month. It opens at half five, as the light starts to go, and stays open through the sunset. There are no courses and no portions — go back as often as you like.

To begin

On arrival

  • Shell lei & welcome pour

    A lilikoʻi cooler or a dry sparkling wine, handed to you on arrival.

  • Poke ʻAhi

    Yellowfin cut that morning, with shoyu and sweet onion.

  • Lomi salmon Lomi lomi

    Salted salmon worked through with tomato and onion, served very cold.

    Gluten free

  • Boiled peanuts

    Simmered in Hawaiian salt.

    Vegan

From the imu and the grill

The pit is opened at a quarter past four

  • Kālua pig Kālua puaʻa

    One whole pig, salted and cooked underground on hot stones, pulled by hand.

  • Taro leaf in coconut Lūʻau

    Young taro leaves stewed slowly in coconut cream until they collapse. The dish the feast is named after.

    Vegan

  • Chicken long rice

    Ginger broth, bean thread noodles, shredded chicken. The mildest thing on the table.

  • Salt-baked sweet potato ʻUala

    Purple sweet potato, baked alongside the pig and split open at the table.

    Vegan

  • Impossible burger

    Plant-based patty, grilled to order at the end of the line, with Maui onion, lettuce and a taro bun. Our standing vegan main.

    Vegan

Alongside

On the long table

  • Poi

    Pounded taro, fresh. Served alongside the salt fish and the pig.

  • Breadfruit ʻUlu

    Roasted in the coals until the skin blackens, then scooped out and dressed with coconut.

    Vegan

  • Grilled sweet onion

    Halved, charred over what is left of the fire, finished with lime.

    Vegan

  • Green papaya salad

    Shredded green papaya, chilli, lime, peanut. Sharp, to cut the richness.

    Vegan

From the bar

One drink included, cards after that

  • Lilikoʻi cooler

    Passion fruit, lime and soda.

    Non-alcoholic

  • Mai tai

    Rum, lime, orgeat. Not sweet — ask if you would prefer the sweeter version.

  • Sparkling wine

    By the glass or the bottle to the table.

  • Beer

    Two from the island and one from the mainland.

To finish

Once the torches are lit

  • Coconut pudding Haupia

    Cut into squares and served cold in a ti leaf.

    Vegan

  • Pineapple, grilled

    Over the last of the coals, with lime and a little chilli salt.

    Vegan

  • Kona coffee

    Poured from about eight o’clock.

  • Okolehao & lilikoʻi

    Ti-root spirit and passion fruit, over ice.

    Bar · 21+
